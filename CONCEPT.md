# Merzavtsy concept

## Core promise

Merzavtsy is a persistent Ethereum life simulation in which **one account has one creature**. Birth creates a locked identity tied to the account; the creature is not a tradable collectible and cannot be transferred or burned by ordinary users.

The game turns a wallet's finalized public activity into biography rather than money. Contract calls, deployments, counterparties, selectors, peer encounters, periods of inactivity and later awakenings become bounded inputs to personality drift, needs, XP, stages, mutations, scars and relationships.

## What progression means

Progression is intentionally non-financial. XP and levels unlock memory/appearance capacity and richer autonomous intentions; they do not represent a redeemable token, yield claim or right to extract value from other players. Repetitive activity is subject to diminishing returns, while long-term variety and biography matter more than raw transaction count.

The creature keeps eight canonical personality axes and bounded needs. Age, activity history, permanent mutations and scars shape its visible interpretation without rewriting the original genome-derived baseline. Five life stages require both chronology and accumulated biography.

## Autonomous life

A Merzavets does not wait for a user to click every action. Permissionless `lifeTick` calls let the creature rest, wander, seek company, mock a rival, groom or hide according to deterministic state. A cooldown bounds how often ticks can mutate state. Hibernating creatures restrict autonomous social behavior until verified wallet activity wakes them.

## Social biography

Relationships are directional: A→B can differ from B→A. Affinity and trust are signed bounded values; fear, respect, envy and rivalry are bounded unsigned values. Friendship, rivalry and betrayal are irreversible biography milestones once earned. Registered-wallet encounters can be attested by the oracle, while explicit owner-triggered social actions remain non-financial game interactions.

## Mutations and scars

Mutations are deterministic consequences of canonical activity patterns and social history. Some require age, level or earlier mutations. Scars record irreversible biography such as first deployment, long sleep, first mutation, first rivalry, old-account age and a rare canonical mutation combination. They are not purchasable boosts.

## What Merzavtsy deliberately is not

The MVP has no project token, NFT marketplace, staking, yield, gambling, paid stat boost, lootbox, custody service or arbitrary oracle call primitive. The oracle can attest bounded gameplay facts; it is not permitted to move user ETH or ERC-20 assets. The submitter pays gas for protocol operations but does not gain oracle-signing authority.

## Design goal

The desired feeling is that a creature gradually becomes a strange, legible reflection of an account's public Ethereum history. The interesting output is an accumulated character and biography, not a farmable financial reward.
